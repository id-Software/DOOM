# M5c: sound effects matched to the genuine engine, and the face-glance gap closed: reference investigation

Phase 1 note for milestone M5c, which gives the playable build Doom's sound effects. Which sounds start, on which channel, at what volume, stereo position and pitch is to be identical to linuxdoom-1.10 tic for tic, and the mixed samples are to match the original mixer under a declared mixing schedule. With sound starts modelled, the status bar face's idle glance is to match a vanilla session with sound, which closes decision S15 of [M5b](M5B_REFERENCE_INVESTIGATION.md#4-owner-decisions-s1s23-with-recommendations). Music, menus and their sounds, the automap, intermission and level-to-level play, save and load, sound options screens and the external `sndserver` process are out of scope.
