## Notes on the st_stuff.c rows

- **Q-st_stuff-123**: an accident with no effect while the two values are equal.
- **Q-st_stuff-807**: merges ouch-face-fires-on-health-gain, face-ouch-test-reversed, ouch-face-reversed-health-test and face-ouch-test-has-reversed-operands.
- **Q-st_stuff-816**: merges face-reads-removed-attacker, status-face-reads-freed-attacker and attacker-pointer-read-after-free.
- **Q-st_stuff-819**: the C comment at 830 reads "confusing, aint it?".
- **Q-st_stuff-992**: M5C §1.4.1 item 6 notes that tools/reference/m3h9c_stubs.c:16, m5a_stubs.c:28 and m5b_stubs.c:40 cite "st_stuff.c:991"; the draw is at 992.
- **Q-st_stuff-1268**: from damage-on-first-tic-shows-ouch-face and the first-tic parts of ouch-face-fires-on-health-gain and face-ouch-test-has-reversed-operands; kept apart from the reversed test because it shows on damage, not on a gain.
- **Q-st_stuff-1292**: an out-of-bounds pointer formation (undefined in C) without a read.
- **Q-st_stuff-384**: The stale-face example is labelled an inference in M5B.
- **Q-st_stuff-816**: COMPAT adds that in one state (dead player, attacker retired) the Rust refusal is wider than the original's read.
