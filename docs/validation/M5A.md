# M5a evidence record

Observed results of the M5a milestone (a faithful Rust software renderer, pixel-identical to
linuxdoom-1.10's `R_RenderPlayerView`). Only observations appear here. The
[M5a investigation](../M5A_REFERENCE_INVESTIGATION.md) owns the source facts, the decisions R1-R16
(§5, confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-15) and the plan; [testing](../TESTING.md#m5a-synthetic-renderer-rooms)
owns the commands. Exact review state and CI belong to GitHub.

**Scope of the record.** Phase 2 step 1 (2026-11-15, America/Los_Angeles): the synthetic graphics,
the synthetic rooms, the M5a genuine runner and the genuine goldens. Phase 2 step 2a (2026-11-16):
the Rust renderer's foundation (tables, view setup, resources, render state, view shadow) and the
comparison harness ([below](#rust-renderer-step-2a)). No IWAD was read in this step. Address-checked runs used
`ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=0`: LeakSanitizer was disabled, not passed. No timing or benchmark was
measured.

## Synthetic graphics, rooms and runner (CI evidence)

  under `~/scratch` (ext4).

- 16 renderer sanitizer adapters on the overflow and address builds only: `r_main.c:484`, `r_main.c:485`,
  `r_segs.c:266`, `r_segs.c:402`, `r_segs.c:603`, `r_segs.c:604`, `r_plane.c:438`, `r_plane.c:439`,

## Proofs on the synthetic runs

Items: the 20 rooms and the 22 scripts of both M4b manifests, all with `m5a_gfx.wad` loaded after the
map WAD. View configurations: screenblocks 11, 10 and 9, detail 0 (decision R2). Command:

```sh
python3 tools/reference/m5a_reference.py --work ~/scratch/m5a/ref --update-events --pngs --selftest
python3 tools/reference/m5a_reference.py --work ~/scratch/m5a/ref --check --pngs
```

**(a) Rendering does not change the game.** For all 42 items the never-render dump set of the M5a strict
build equals the committed M4b digest (22 M4b scripts) or the frozen M4b runner's dump set on the same
WAD, graphics and demo (20 rooms). For all 42 items and the three configurations, the render-every dump
sets (with `ML_MAPPED`, bit 256 of `L.flags`, masked; decision R5) of every variant equal the
never-render set snapshot for snapshot (through the last dumped tic for the three runs that end at a
renderer limit). The masking is needed: the frame streams record newly mapped lines on every item.

**(b) Variant identity.** For every item and configuration the strict, overflow and address frame streams
and view records are byte-identical through the last tic every variant rendered. Tracker-flagged
frames are compared on their masked digest; only M5SHORT has them (below). Frames per configuration:
8,180 at screenblocks 11, 8,180 at 10 and 8,192 at 9 (the M4b scripts 5,235 each; the rooms 2,945, 2,945
and 2,957: OPENINGS and SOLIDSEGS end later at screenblocks 9). strict32 (local only, `-m32`, genuine
text, no adapter at all): `m5a_reference.py --variants strict --strict32` found the strict and strict32
frame streams, view records and dump sets identical for all 42 items in the three configurations (it
writes nothing; with only these two builds the comparison also covers the last frame of SOLIDSEGS, which
the address build does not finish, hence 8,181, 8,181 and 8,194 frames).

**(c) Goldens.** Committed from the strict runs: `tests/reference/m5a_frames/<LUMP>.sb<N>.frames` (126
files: one F row per frame with its SHA-256, plus the mapped-line, tracker and counter rows),
`tests/reference/m5a_views/<LUMP>.view` (42 files, DOOM-M5A-VIEW-v1), `tests/reference/m5a_results.json`
and 26 PNGs of selected room frames in `tests/reference/m5a_png/` (synthetic palette 0; tracker-flagged
columns drawn as index 0).

**(d) The frame comparison can fail.** `--selftest` flips one pixel, (123, 77), after
`R_RenderPlayerView` of tic 20 of M5VIEW (screenblocks 10); the comparison reported the first differing
frame at tic 20 and the first differing pixel at (123, 77): pass.

## Limit rooms

| Room | Bound | Observed (strict; the same tic in every configuration unless noted) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VPLANES | MAXVISPLANES 128 | 126 visplanes in the frame of tic 48; at tic 49 `R_FindPlane` calls I_Error "no more visplanes" (r_plane.c:245-246), the same in all three variants and configurations |
| DSEGS | MAXDRAWSEGS 256 | 256 drawsegs and the first dropped `R_StoreWallRange` call at tic 51 (53 at screenblocks 9); 29 frames with drops (25 at screenblocks 9); the run ends normally |
| VSPRITES | MAXVISSPRITES 128 | 128 vissprites at tic 49; `overflowsprite` first written at tic 50; 32 frames with a drop; the run ends normally |
| OPENINGS | MAXOPENINGS 20,480 | 20,391 openings in the frame of tic 39 (20,394 at tic 48 at screenblocks 9); at tic 40 (49) `R_StoreWallRange` writes past `openings[]` |
| SOLIDSEGS | MAXSEGS 32 | a peak of 31 ranges before the write (tic 13 at screenblocks 11 and 10); at tic 15 (19 at screenblocks 9) `R_ClipSolidWallSegment` writes past `solidsegs[]` |

At the two writes past an array the strict and overflow builds report the limit witness (an O row:
OpeningOverflow, SolidSegOverflow) and the driver ends the run after that frame (end=refused); the
address build stops inside the frame with "AddressSanitizer: global-buffer-overflow" in
`R_StoreWallRange` or `R_ClipSolidWallSegment`. Every frame before that tic is identical in the three
builds, with one exception at screenblocks 9 in SOLIDSEGS: there the address build stops one frame
earlier, at tic 18, on an 8-byte READ past `solidsegs[]` in `R_ClipSolidWallSegment`. With the list full
(32 entries), the crunch loop `while (next++ != newend) *++start = *next;` (r_bsp.c:178-182) copies the
element one past the last valid entry, which here is `solidsegs[32]`; the copy lands after the
`0x7fffffff` sentinel, where no later search looks, so the strict and strict32 frame of tic 18 does not
depend on the bytes read (strict and strict32 agree on it). The driver's witness sees writes only, so the
strict run reports SolidSegOverflow at tic 19. The committed goldens stop at tic 17 for that run (the last
frame all three builds rendered). Whether the Rust renderer refuses at the read (tic 18) or at the first
write (tic 19) is not settled by R3 and is left open (see the investigation's status line). At screenblocks
11 and 10 the first access past the array is the write at tic 15 in every build. The strict OPENINGS frame then reaches the `R_DrawPlanes` I_Error "opening overflow" with a
count that depends on the overwritten memory (it differed between builds during authoring), so only
the witness is recorded.

## Rust renderer, step 2a

Observed 2026-11-16 (America/Los_Angeles) on the synthetic items only; no IWAD was read. Commands
are in [testing](../TESTING.md#m5a-synthetic-renderer-rooms).

- **Tables.** `m5a_tables.py --check` passed: `crates/doom-render/src/tables.rs` holds
  `finetangent[4096]` then `finesine[10240]` in one array (the genuine contiguous layout) and
  `tantoangle[2049]`, copied from linuxdoom-1.10/tables.c.
- **Resources.** For all 42 items the genuine global lump list (map WAD, `m5a_gfx.wad`, the demo
  lump) loads: textures, composites, flats, sprite lumps, the 138 sprite definitions, the
  registered-mode switch list, the animations, `SKY1` and `F_SKY1`; every map's side textures and
  sector flats resolve.
- **View shadow (decision R6).** `tests/view_shadow.rs`: 8,238 genuine view records (every record
  of the 42 items, the load included) compared; every field matched in every record: `viewx`,
  `viewy`, `viewz`, `viewangle`, `viewheight`, `deltaviewheight`, `bob`, `onground`, `extralight`,
  `fixedcolormap`, `invisibility`, `ps0state`, `ps0tics`, `ps0sx`, `ps0sy`, `ps1state`, `ps1tics`,
  `ps1sx`, `ps1sy`, `sector`, `lightlevel` and the flat/texture translations (A rows): 8,238 of
  8,238 each. No Rust run ended before its record. Coverage limit: in these records `viewheight`
  never differs from `VIEWHEIGHT`, `deltaviewheight` is always 0 and `onground` is 0 only at the
  load, so the step-up, hard-landing and death paths are not yet compared (investigation §7, item 33).
- **Harness.** `tests/frames.rs`: 24,552 genuine frames (8,180 at screenblocks 11, 8,180 at 10,
  8,192 at 9) reached and compared; 0 matched, 0 mismatched, 0 non-comparable, 24,552 refused with
  `NotImplemented("R_RenderBSPNode")` (no drawing code yet). The self-test (planted pixel; change
  inside and outside a flagged column; whole-frame fallback) passed.
- **Reporter.** `m5a_compare --only M5PEG,TELE1` with the strict runner wrote the genuine and Rust
  PNG pair of each first non-matching frame (tic 0 in every configuration) to `~/scratch/m5a/diff`
  (local only); a PNG re-read with Python's `zlib` passed every chunk CRC and inflated to the
  expected 64,200 bytes.

## Findings

- **Step 2a: a new `tools/reference/generate*.py` breaks a frozen check.** The first push of step 2a
  named the table generator `generate_m5a_tables.py`; CI's source suite then failed
  `m3f5_reference.py --check` on `tests/reference/m3f5_provenance.json`, because the frozen M3f3
  harness copies every `tools/reference/generate*.py` into its workspace (`m3f3_reference.py:72`).
  Renamed to `m5a_tables.py`; the same check then passed locally through `run_reference.py`. New tools
  must not use that prefix.

- **The builder chain writes every seg with angle 0 and offset 0** (`m3h6_geometry.room`). Gameplay never
  reads them; the renderer does (`P_LoadSegs`, p_setup.c:181-182; r_segs.c:401, 635). The rooms rewrite
  both fields (`m5a_wad.fix_segs`). The frozen M4b WADs keep them, so their frames are the genuine
  renderer's output for those bytes, with walls mapped as if every seg faced east.
- **`P_LoadThings` stops at the first DoomEd 65 outside commercial mode** (p_setup.c:317-337: `break` out of
  the THINGS loop, not a skip). An early SPRITES room with a chaingunner lost every later THING; the room
  uses no 65. Whether wire 23/24 spawns a 65 on a registered map was not checked in this step.
- **Low detail** is refused (status 8); no comparison was built for it.
- **Invulnerability** (DoomEd 2022, `INVERSECOLORMAP`) is not admitted by the simulation, so no room
  reaches fixed colour map 32.
- The light-amplification flicker at the end of the power (4,200 tics) is not in AMP; the room shows
  `fixedcolormap` 0 and 1.
